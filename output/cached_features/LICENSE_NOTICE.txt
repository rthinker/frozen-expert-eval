This derived numerical cache contains frozen-expert features, logits, labels,
sample identifiers and split provenance. It contains no raw media or detector
checkpoint weights. Its data have not changed since the reviewed release.

The cache transformation consists of extracting numerical representations and
predictions with frozen DF40 detectors; raw images are not redistributed.

INTENDED USE AND LIMITS

Intended uses are non-commercial methodological and educational evaluation of
frozen-expert composition. The reported evidence does not validate deployment,
forensic attribution or surveillance. Re-identification of individuals falls
outside the intended use of this cache. Respect applicable upstream terms and
privacy rights.

See README.md / DATASET_CARD.md for splits, overlap, integrity limitations,
intended uses and citation; croissant.json for metadata; and CHECKSUMS.txt for
the unchanged data-file hashes. CACHE_PROVENANCE.json binds those hashes
and this current notice separately. The pinned review revision retains
its own notice. The current download instructions preserve this notice.
